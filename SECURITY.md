# Security

To report a security vulnerability in StepSAFE, write to August@Outlier.Systems. We confirm we received your report
within three business days, and we tell you what we found and when it is fixed. Please give us time to fix it before
you share it publicly. We will agree a disclosure date with you.

The full vulnerability disclosure policy, with what is in scope and the promise not to take legal action against
good-faith research, is at https://unitium-safe.vercel.app/security.html. The same contact is in
https://unitium-safe.vercel.app/.well-known/security.txt, and https://unitium-safe.vercel.app/trust reads these
promises from the live site.

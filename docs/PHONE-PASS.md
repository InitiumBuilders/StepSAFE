# A pass on a real phone

Everything built on 2026-11-11 was measured in Chrome's phone mode and in WebKit at iPhone size. Neither is a
phone. This is ten minutes on a real one, with what to look for at each step. A screenshot of anything that looks
off, with the name of the page, is all that is needed back.

Open https://unitium-safe.vercel.app in Safari on an iPhone, or Chrome on an Android phone.

## 1. The tab bar

- Tap Ground, Chapters, The Practice, Modux Models. Each opens, and the light over the tabs slides to it.
- Tap More. A sheet rises with Respect, Trust and Rank, Find, and eight places. Tap above the sheet to close it.
- Look at the bottom edge. The bar should sit clear of the home indicator, with no page showing under it.
- Scroll a long page. The bar should stay put while Safari's own toolbar shrinks.

## 2. Back

- Open Chapters, then a chapter, scroll down, then open a Step.
- Swipe back from the left edge. You should be on the chapter, as far down as you were.
- Swipe back again. You should be on Chapters, still inside the site.
- Open Find from More, then swipe back. Find should close and the page under it should stay.

## 3. A room that keeps its place

- On Ground, under the welcome, tap Take one. Answer the decision. The page should move so the outcome starts
  near the top of the screen. Then go back. The card should name another room.
- On The Practice, tap Take one. Answer the decision. Tap Another one.
- Go back to The Practice, open a room, tap Take the chair, and take two decisions. Under Continue it should say
  your place is kept.
- Close the tab completely, or swipe the browser away. Open the site again.
- Ground should lead with the room, In progress, and so should The Practice. Continue should open it on the
  inject you left.

## 4. Reading

- Read a Step to its end and seal it. A card for the Step after it should appear, whole above the tab bar. Tap
  Next Step.
- Open the chapter THE KEEPER and scroll past its Pages. The Practice should name the room that stands on it.
- Open the Step "Stocks, Flows, and the Bathtub You Are Standing In". Its table should read as cards, one for each
  row.
- Open any Step with code. The code should wrap.
- Nothing on any page should scroll sideways, apart from the A to Z strip in the glossary and StudyMode's row of
  tabs.

## 5. A link

- On a room's brief, tap Share this room. The phone's own share sheet should open with the link.
- Send it to yourself and open it in a private tab. You should meet the welcome, take a handle, and land in
  that room.
- Reload any page. It should open on the same place.

## 6. Installed

- iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the icon. The clock and battery should not sit on top of
  the page's first line, and the tab bar should clear the home indicator.
- Android: install it from the browser menu, then press and hold the icon. Four shortcuts should appear.
- Android only: a decision in a room should give a short tap in the hand. iPhone Safari does not offer this.

## 7. Turned sideways

- Turn the phone on its side. The tab bar gives way to a row of tabs along the top.

Report a Security Problem

StepSAFE welcomes reports of security problems from anyone. This page says what is in scope, how to report, and what we ask and offer in return.

Where To Send It

Email August@Outlier.Systems. The same address is in /.well-known/security.txt.

Tell us what you found, where it is, the steps that show it, and what someone could do with it. Please leave out any personal data you came across.

In Scope

  • unitium-safe.vercel.app, the StepSAFE web app.
  • Its one server function, /api/seva, which sends a guide's conversation to Claude.

Out Of Scope

  • The outside services the app can contact, listed on the privacy page. Report those to their owners.
  • Denial-of-service testing, or anything that slows the site for others.
  • Social engineering, phishing or physical attacks.
  • Reports from automated scanners with no shown impact.

What Ships To Your Browser

The open-source packages and font files StepSAFE ships are listed, with their versions and checksums, in sbom.cdx.json, a software bill of materials in the CycloneDX format. The site's content security policy lets a page run scripts from this site only.

StepSAFE's own record reads these promises from the site in your browser and says which hold, with StepSAFE's gaps listed first.

What We Ask

  • Test only with your own data, in your own browser.
  • Stop as soon as you have shown the problem, and report it.
  • Give us time to fix it before you share it publicly. We will agree a date with you.

What We Offer

  • We will confirm we received your report within three business days.
  • We will tell you what we found and when it is fixed.
  • If you follow this policy in good faith, we will not take or support legal action against you for your research.
  • With your permission, we will credit you when the fix ships.

This policy is based on the vulnerability disclosure policy template that CISA, the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency, publishes.
